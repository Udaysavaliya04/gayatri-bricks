Key Construction Advantages

Practical benefits for contractors, civil engineers, and property developers.

0.25 kg CO₂ / Brick

Eliminates kiln firing, avoiding ~50–60 tonnes of CO₂ emissions and protecting over 1,000 m³ of fertile topsoil per residential complex.

40–50% Mortar Reduction

Uniform hydraulic pressing ensures flat surfaces and sharp edges, cutting joint mortar and plaster consumption nearly in half.

No 24-Hour Water Soaking

Does not require soaking tanks prior to masonry laying. Light water sprinkling before placement is sufficient, saving site labor and water.

Direct Gypsum Plastering

Smooth, even brick face allows direct application of gypsum plaster without needing a backing coat of lime plaster.

30–35% Steel & Load Savings

Optimized unit mass (~3.2 kg) reduces overall structural dead weight, enabling civil engineers to optimize column size and steel reinforcement.

High Fire & Thermal Insulation

Provides solid fire resistance and low thermal conductivity, keeping building interiors up to 3°C cooler during summer months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from contractors and project developers.

What is the standard size of your fly ash bricks?

Our bricks are manufactured to 230 × 110 × 75 mm (approximately 9 in × 4 in × 3 in), which is the IS standard size for Fly Ash Bricks used across India.

Are your bricks BIS / IS certified?

Yes. Our bricks comply with IS 12894:2002. Every batch is tested for compressive strength (minimum 10.5 N/mm²

What is the minimum order quantity?

We supply orders of all sizes. Call +91 90678 28575 to discuss pricing for your specific quantity. Bulk orders receive competitive wholesale pricing.

Do fly ash bricks need 24-hour water soaking before use?

No. Unlike red clay bricks, fly ash bricks only need light water sprinkling immediately before placing. This saves time and water on the jobsite.

Can I apply gypsum plaster directly on fly ash bricks?

Yes. The smooth, uniform surface allows direct gypsum plaster application without a backing coat of lime plaster, saving material and finishing time.

How much mortar is saved compared to clay bricks?

Precise dimensions and sharp edges reduce mortar consumption by 40–50% compared to irregular red clay bricks. Thinner joints also reduce overall plaster cost.
