Detailed Advantage Breakdown

Filter advantages by engineering domain to review laboratory test data & site benefits.

Strength

>10.5 N/mm² Load Bearing Strength

Hydraulically pressed under 150-ton automated pressure to achieve BIS Class 10 compliance. Exceeds standard red clay bricks (3.5–5 N/mm²) by over 2.5x, making it ideal for load-bearing multi-story structures.

Compressive Rating 10.5 N/mm² vs 3.5 N/mm²
Precision

±1 mm Uniform Dimensional Precision

Manufactured using machine-machined steel molds. Eliminates irregular brick dimensions, lopsided courses, and wavy wall lines, ensuring 100% true vertical alignment on site.

Dimensional Uniformity 99.2% Accuracy
ROI & Cost

35% Reduction in Plaster & Mortar

Smooth plane faces and razor-sharp edges allow masons to apply a thin single-coat plaster (10-12mm) instead of 20-25mm double coats on red clay bricks. Saves 30-35% on cement and gypsum plaster.

Plaster Material Saved 35% Direct Savings
Thermal

Superior Thermal Insulation (0.90 W/mK)

Low thermal conductivity rating insulates interiors against extreme summer heat in Gujarat. Keeps building interiors up to 3°C to 5°C cooler, reducing air conditioning electricity bills.

Indoor Temp Reduction -4°C Ambient Cooling
Protection

100% Termite & Pest Immunity

Formulated from an inorganic mineral matrix of fly ash, lime, gypsum, and stone dust. Completely immune to subterranean termites, borers, or organic pest nesting for lifetime peace of mind.

Pest Immunity Rating 100% Inorganic
Durability

Low Water Absorption (<12%) & No Efflorescence

Controlled water absorption prevents the leaching of soluble salts (shora/efflorescence). Prevents white salt streaks, wall dampness, paint peeling, and interior plaster flaking.

Efflorescence Resistance Zero Salt Streaking
Weathering

Monsoon & Coastal Weather Resilience

Resists heavy monsoon rain cycles, freeze-thaw degradation, and salty coastal atmospheres without crumbling, softening, or expanding. Ideal for industrial corridors and coastal Gujarat.

Weathering Life Rating 100+ Years
Sustainability

Eco-Friendly & Topsoil Conservation

Uses industrial fly ash bypass, preserving fertile agricultural topsoil used in red clay brick kilns. Requires no coal firing, zero CO2 furnace emissions, and qualifies for IGBC Green Building points.

Topsoil Preserved 100% Topsoil Saved
